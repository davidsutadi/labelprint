Privacy Policy

Label Printer (id.hsi.labelprint) · Last updated: 18 October 2026

HomePrivacy Policy

Summary

Label Printer works on your device. We do not run a server, we do not collect your data, and we do not use advertising or analytics. The only time data leaves your phone is when you choose to turn on Google Drive sync, and then it goes to your own Google Drive.

Data the app handles

  • Label PDFs and print history. The PDFs you open and the history of printed labels (tracking numbers, courier, names and addresses shown on the labels) are stored locally on your device so you can reprint and search them.
  • Camera. Used only to scan QR codes and barcodes on labels. Scanning runs on the device; images are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth. Used only to connect to your thermal printer and send the label to print.
  • Storage access. Used to open and save label PDFs and backups that you select.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you sign in with Google to turn on sync, the app asks for the drive.file permission. This lets the app see and manage only the files it creates itself in your Google Drive, or files you pick. It cannot read the rest of your Drive. The app uses this access only to upload backups of renamed label PDFs, download them again, and sync the print log between your own devices.

Your Google access is used only for these features. We do not receive it, share it, sell it, or use it for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can stop sync at any time by removing the app's access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. Data in your Google Drive is governed by your Google account and Google's own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Backups in Google Drive stay until you delete them from Drive.

Children

The app is built for sellers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
